TypoGraph

Draw every character in your handwriting — digitally or on paper — then export a real TTF font.

Two ways to work

  • Draw digitally: Click a character in the chart (or use ← → to step through), sketch on the canvas, then move to the next glyph — it saves automatically.
  • Procreate / paper workflow: Download the blank chart → fill it out in Procreate (or on paper, then scan) → upload the filled PNG here. Registration marks in the corners let the tool auto-align the import precisely.
  • Both paths feed into the same font — mix and match freely. Hit Download font (TTF) when ready.

How to use the Procreate workflow:
  1. Download the blank chart below — it includes corner registration marks.
  2. Open it in Procreate (or print & scan). Draw each character inside its cell using black ink on white. Don't draw over the corner marks.
  3. Export as PNG at any resolution — keep the full canvas, don't crop.
  4. Upload filled chart below. The tool reads the registration marks to auto-align, then extracts every cell at full quality.
