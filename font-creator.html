TypoGraph

Map every character to your handwriting with a simple chart. Draw, preview, and export a sheet you can use anywhere.

How it works

  • Pick a character, sketch it on the pad, then save it to the chart.
  • Fill out each box to build a full alphabet (upper/lowercase, numbers, punctuation).
  • Download the filled chart to print, share, or trace into a full font editor.

Draw a glyph

Select a character from the chart, sketch your version, then save. Brush is pressureless to keep things simple.
